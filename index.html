TIC TAC TOE

HOW TO PLAY


Tic Tac Toe is played on a 3×3 grid.

Player X goes first.

Players take turns placing their mark (X or O) in any empty square.

The goal is to be the first to get 3 in a row:
- Horizontally
- Vertically
- Or diagonally

If all 9 squares are filled and no one gets 3 in a row, the game ends in a draw.
